Four steps, running before you're awake

Setu sits between your brokerage account and the market, doing the watching and the clicking for you.

01

Connect your broker

Link an account through an official trading API. Setu never asks for or stores your login password.

02

Stream Nifty 50 data

Daily and intraday prices are pulled continuously, with history backfilled for testing your rule before it goes live.

03

Define your crossover

Choose the short and long moving-average windows, or bring your own indicator. This is the only decision you make.

04

Auto-enter and auto-exit

When the rule fires, Setu places the order — an index ETF buy on a golden cross, a square-off on a death cross.

Before you automate anything

Worth reading slowly, not just accepting.

Read this before connecting a live account

  • Groww has no public trading API. Tools that log in on your behalf or scrape session tokens sit outside Groww's terms of service, can break without warning, and put your credentials at risk. Setu only executes through brokers with an official, documented API.
  • A backtest is not a guarantee. A moving-average crossover that performed well on past Nifty 50 data can still lose money going forward, especially in sideways or choppy markets where it whipsaws between buy and sell signals.
  • Automation does not remove risk, it removes hesitation. Set position sizing and a maximum loss you're comfortable with before switching auto-execution on.
  • This page is a product concept, not investment advice. Algorithmic trading in India operates under SEBI and exchange guidelines that apply to you as the account holder, regardless of what places the order.
